Smashing Magazine’s in-person conferences are back and returning to the wonderful city of [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Freiburg](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026) next month, September 7–10. In you didn’t know it, Freiburg is where it all started for Smashing Magazine! It’s their home turf and the conferences, if you haven’t been to one before, are *always* worth it.

Freiburg is a two-day single-track conference with a bunch of speakers I know you know, including a few new faces. Folks like:

- [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Jake Archibald](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/speakers/jake-archibald)
- [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Lyra Rebane](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/speakers/lyra-rebane) (who absolutely wowed me [<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-youtube"/>in a Smashing Meets session](https://youtu.be/4GzHqfAQQb4))
- [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Hidde de Vries](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/speakers/hidde-de-vries)
- [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Sarah Thompson](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/speakers/sarah-thompson) (a recent Smashing [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>“Person of the Week”](https://smashingmagazine.com/person-of-the-week/sarah-thompson/))

…and a [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>mystery guest](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/speakers/mystery-speaker)! Which always winds up being some incredible rock star worth the price of admission alone.

Can’t make the event? [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Follow it online!](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/online) But, really, [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>register for it](https://smashingconf.com/freiburg-2026/registration) if you can. CSS-Tricks readers like you even get [a generous 15% discount](https://ti.to/smashingmagazine/smashingconf-freiburg-2026/discount/welovecommunity).
