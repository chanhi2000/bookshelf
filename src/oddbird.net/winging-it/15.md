Responsive Typography has been around for at least a decade in various forms, but has become even more popular with tools like [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Utopia.fyi](https://utopia.fyi/), [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Fluid.style](https://fluid.style/), [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Typetura](https://github.com/Typetura/), and more – all relying on the latest CSS units and math functions. But there are still a lot of questions worth asking.
