::: info Opera announcing their transition (<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-opera"/><code>my.opera.com</code>)

> Consumers will initially notice better site compatibility, especially with mobile-facing sites - many of which have only been tested in WebKit browsers.

~~[Opera announcing their transition](http://my.opera.com/ODIN/blog/300-million-users-and-move-to-webkit)~~

:::

Site compatibility is something Opera has struggled with—last year they took the controversial decision to add support for the webkit vendor prefix, along with their own prefix, which undoubtedly ~~[<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-opera"/>introduced some confusion](http://my.opera.com/ODIN/blog/2012/08/10/css-vendor-prefixes-in-opera-12-50-snapshots)~~, but more importantly risked undermining the purpose of vendor prefixes. [**Craig Buckler wrote for SitePoint**](/sitepoint.com/w3c-css-webkit-prefix-crisis.md):

::: info (<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/><code>sitepoint.com</code>)

> Taking the decision to it’s logical extreme, all vendors would support every prefix but any implementation differences would render the CSS property useless.

:::

Although competition is healthy, Opera’s transition is promising for Opera users as well as developers; Opera users will experience a less broken browsing experience, while we’re avoiding the risk of further fragmenting the web. And hopefully, we’ll see WebKit grow even stronger. [<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-opera"/>Opera, near the end of their announcement](http://my.opera.com/ODIN/blog/300-million-users-and-move-to-webkit):

::: info Opera announcing their transition (<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-opera"/><code>my.opera.com</code>)

> In the last few weeks ~~[<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-safari"/>we’ve contacted the Webkit project](https://lists.webkit.org/pipermail/webkit-dev/2013-February/023820.html)~~, and contributing organisations, to discuss our intentions to work with them to make WebKit even better. By contributing patches back to WebKit, we’ll enhance standards compliance across a range of browsers, not just Opera.

:::
