[<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Icônes](https://icones.js.org/) is an amazing icon resource site. There are tens of thousands of free-to-use icons which are easy to browse and find. I guess it’s like an alternate UI to [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Iconify](https://iconify.design/), making it ultra-fast to get the icon in a format you want it in, like a quick download of SVG or PNG, copying to your clipboard in one of those formats, or as a basic component in many of the most popular frameworks.

![](https://i0.wp.com/frontendmasters.com/blog/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/Screenshot-2024-05-06-at-3.07.54%E2%80%AFPM.png?resize=794%2C828&quality=80&ssl=1)

I still love [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-glob e"/>The Noun Project](https://thenounproject.com/) a bunch, but it costs bucks, doesn’t seem to have innovated much (like it doesn’t have as many nice exporting options), and doesn’t group icons as well.

Jeez, [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>Anthony Fu](https://antfu.me/) has done some incredible open-source projects!

Oh, and hey, if the idea of exporting to framework components is appealing to you, check out [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>iconbundler](https://iconbundler.com/) which can take a whole bunch of them and do it for you right from the browser. I tend to use [<VPIcon icon="fas fa-globe"/>SVGR](https://react-svgr.com/), though, to automate it.
