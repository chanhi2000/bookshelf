Master the entire UI/UX design workflow.

We just published a comprehensive six-hour course on the [<FontIcon icon="fa-brands fa-free-code-camp"/>freeCodeCamp.org](http://freeCodeCamp.org) YouTube channel that takes you from Figma basics to professional-grade, high-fidelity prototyping. Through hands-on, real-world projects like travel and food delivery apps, you’ll gain practical experience with advanced components, Auto Layout, and 3D elements to build a production-ready design portfolio. Wish Naik developed this course.

You’ll learn core Figma tools and advanced UI/UX concepts, including:

- Figma interface, tools, and essential shortcuts
- Professional style guides and color systems
- Low-fidelity wireframes to high-fidelity prototypes
- Components, Auto Layout, and Constraints
- Real-world projects like travel websites, car dealership sites, and food delivery apps
- Advanced techniques such as multi-dimensional components and 3D web elements

Watch the full course on [<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-youtube"/>the freeCodeCamp.org YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/mT_Jjn8RJdo) (6-hour watch).
