The NCA-AIIO certification is an entry-level credential that validates the foundational concepts of AI computing related to infrastructure and operations.

We just posted a course on the [<FontIcon icon="fa-brands fa-free-code-camp"/>freeCodeCamp.org](http://freeCodeCamp.org) YouTube channel that will teach you everything you need to know to pass the certification exam. Andrew Brown from ExamPro developed this course.

Here are the topics covered in this exam:

- Accelerated computing use cases
- AI, machine learning, and deep learning
- GPU architecture
- NVIDIA’s software suite
- Infrastructure and operation considerations for adopting NVIDIA solutions
