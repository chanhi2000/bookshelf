Stop treating Notion like a digital notebook and start using it as a high-level operating system.

We just posted a massive Notion course on the freeCodeCamp.org YouTube channel. This course takes you from a complete beginner to a certified-level power user, taught by a pro who transitioned from law to full-time Notion consulting.

The course is broken down into six clear phases:

- The Basics: Learn the "Software Lego" philosophy—blocks, databases, and visual layouts.
- Productivity: Build a complete task manager and master the new Notion Mail and Calendar.
- Business Systems: Scale Notion for teams with OKRs, department dashboards, and corporate wikis.
- Notion AI: Automate knowledge work and master Context Layer Prompting (CLP).
- Logic & Automation: Dive into Formulas 2.0, Map/Filter functions, and Slack integrations.
- Admin & Security: Manage permissions and protect sensitive data at scale.

Watch the full course [<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-youtube"/>on the freeCodeCamp.org YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/bB5eA7vU9W8) (12-hour watch).
