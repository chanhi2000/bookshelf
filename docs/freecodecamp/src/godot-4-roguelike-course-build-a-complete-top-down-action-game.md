A top-down action roguelite only works when combat feels sharp, rooms feel connected, and every run asks you to trade power for risk. Godot 4 has the tools for that, but most tutorials stop before those systems fit together into a game someone else can open and play.

We just published a comprehensive 10-hour course on the freeCodeCamp.org YouTube channel that will teach you how to build a complete top-down action roguelite in Godot 4.7.2, from an empty project to an exported game. This course is taught by Omar Zaki, creator of Coding Quests, and most lessons include a Your Turn section where you build the next piece yourself before you see the solution.

Here is what the course covers:

- **Dash Combat & I-Frames**<br/>Build weighty movement, aiming, and a dodge with invincibility frames so combat feels fair instead of cheap.
- **Enemies, Telegraphs & a Boss Tell**<br/>Create five enemy types from a shared base, then add a boss with a clear warning before the hard hit lands.
- **Procedural Connected Rooms**<br/>Generate random floors of linked rooms in code, track where you are, and lock a fight until the room is clear.
- **Items, Corruption & Shops**<br/>Stack items into builds, take corruption that trades power for fragility, and risk gold at shops and a gambling altar.
- **Run Loop, Unlocks & Export**<br/>Wire death, permanent unlocks, a title screen, sound, and an export so a stranger can download and play your game.

Watch the full course on [<VPIcon icon="fa-brands fa-youtube"/>the freeCodeCamp.org YouTube channel](https://youtu.be/eHHxRJulX74) (10-hour watch).
